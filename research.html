Supplementation

Creatine Monohydrate Efficacy & Cognitive Impact

Beyond ATP regeneration and cell volumization during resistance training, recent meta-analyses highlight creatine's role in cellular bioenergetics within neural tissue, supporting mental stamina during high-load cognitive work.

Key Takeaway: 5g daily is sufficient. No loading phases, expensive esters, or cycling required.
Nutrition

Protein Distribution vs. Daily Thresholds

Examining muscle protein synthesis (MPS) responses to varying boluses of high-leucine protein. While total daily protein intake remains the primary driver of hypertrophy, evenly distributing intake optimizes anabolism across a 24-hour cycle.

Key Takeaway: Hit your daily protein target consistently via whole-food sources and quality whey; micro-timing is secondary to total volume.

Minimalist Training & Mechanical Tension

Studies comparing high-frequency multi-joint compound movements against exhaustive multi-split routines show equivalent hypertrophic outcomes provided relative intensity (proximity to failure) is high.

Key Takeaway: Master the core lifts. Heavy compound volume beats endless accessory isolation work for foundational progress.
